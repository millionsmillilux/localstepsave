# LocalStep Launchpad

Build a polished, professional startup MVP website for a company called LocalStep — a local jobs and internship marketplace designed specifically for high school and college students.

PRODUCT CONCEPT

LocalStep helps high school and college students discover local jobs, internships, volunteer opportunities, and early-career experiences that are actually relevant to students.

The core problem is that traditional job platforms are not designed around the needs of teenagers and students. Students often have limited work experience, transportation constraints, school schedules, and need opportunities close to where they live or attend school.

LocalStep solves this by creating a student-focused marketplace where:

Students can discover local jobs and internships

Students can filter opportunities by distance, age requirement, job type, schedule, and experience level

Employers can create job listings specifically targeting students

Employers can reach qualified local student applicants

Schools and youth organizations can eventually use the platform to connect students with opportunities

The business model is:

Students: FREE

Employers: Paid job postings and premium placement

Schools/youth organizations: Optional paid partnerships

Future revenue: advertising and sponsored employer placements

The initial MVP should focus heavily on the student experience, while also demonstrating the employer side of the marketplace.
